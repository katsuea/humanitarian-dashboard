**Description**

After more than a decade of conflict, Yemen’s humanitarian crisis continues to deteriorate. Renewed fighting between Houthi forces and government-aligned forces in September 2026 has undermined the relative calm since the 2022 truce. In Taiz, intensified violence and restricted supply routes are worsening civilian suffering.

More than 250,000 people were reportedly displaced across Yemen between 1 August and 17 October. Food and fuel prices have surged, while shortages of flour, medical supplies and fuel are disrupting essential services. Declining water production and damaged sanitation systems further increase the risk of cholera and other diseases.

Widespread food insecurity, displacement and economic collapse, compounded by inadequate humanitarian funding, continue to threaten millions of people.
