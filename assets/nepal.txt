**Description**

The flash floods that struck Nepal on 26 August 2026 caused extensive damage in Rasuwa and neighbouring districts. More than 1,500 people have died and 5,600 remain missing. Approximately 100,000 people have been affected, including over 30,000 children. The disaster destroyed more than 100 bridges, 13 hydropower dams and 23 schools. An estimated 2.2 million tonnes of debris and sediment buried homes, schools, roads and river channels.

Infrastructure damage has severely disrupted transportation, turning journeys that once took minutes into trips of up to 12 hours. Despite these challenges, displaced communities remain determined to return home.

Recovery needs are estimated at \$4.8 billion, equivalent to around 10 per cent of Nepal’s GDP. A flash appeal for \$50 million in immediate support has so far raised only \$24.5 million.
