**Description**

Ukraine continues to face a severe humanitarian crisis due to ongoing hostilities, civilian casualties, infrastructure damage and displacement. Attacks on residential areas and energy facilities have disrupted access to electricity, heating, water, healthcare and other essential services. Vulnerable groups, including older people, people with disabilities and displaced families, face heightened protection and shelter needs.

As winter approaches, humanitarian needs are expected to intensify. UNHCR and its partners continue providing emergency shelter materials, cash assistance, protection services and support for evacuated people. However, insecurity, operational challenges and funding shortages constrain humanitarian operations.

The Ukraine Winter Response Plan (October 2026–March 2027) aims to assist 2.1 million people, requiring US$358.4 million. Priorities include heating assistance, home repairs, essential supplies, healthcare and protection for vulnerable households.
