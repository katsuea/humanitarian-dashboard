**Description**

The DR Congo is experiencing a large and geographically expanding outbreak of Ebola disease caused by Bundibugyo virus, declared on 15 May 2026. As of 20 October 2026, WHO reported 9,704 confirmed cases and 4,687 deaths across 66 health zones in eight provinces, representing a crude case-fatality ratio of 48.3%. Ituri remains the epicentre, while sustained transmission across multiple provinces presents significant challenges for outbreak control. The high number of community deaths highlights gaps in early case detection and timely access to appropriate care. Contact tracing remains below the 95% target, increasing the need to strengthen surveillance and follow-up. 

Imported cases reported in Kenya and Nigeria further underscore the risk of cross-border transmission. Response priorities include rapid case identification, laboratory diagnosis, clinical care, infection prevention and control, safe burials, community engagement and cross-border coordination.
