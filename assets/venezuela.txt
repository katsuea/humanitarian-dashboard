**Description**

Venezuela continues to face a severe humanitarian crisis driven by prolonged socioeconomic hardship, inflation, food insecurity, and limited access to essential services. Nearly 8 million Venezuelans have left the country, while vulnerable communities face persistent challenges accessing healthcare, safe water, food, education, and protection. 

Major earthquakes in June 2026 further intensified humanitarian needs, damaging homes, hospitals, schools, and critical infrastructure. The UN and its humanitarian partners expanded their 2026 response plan to target 6.2 million people, requiring approximately US$931 million. 

However, only 43.2% of the revised funding requirement had been secured by September 22, 2026. Children, women, older people, Indigenous communities, and people with disabilities remain particularly vulnerable. Sustained international funding and coordinated humanitarian assistance are essential to address immediate needs and support recovery.
