**Description**

Sudan is experiencing one of the world’s largest humanitarian crises following the outbreak of conflict between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) in April 2023.

Humanitarian conditions are particularly critical in Darfur and Kordofan, where intensified fighting has increased civilian suffering and restricted humanitarian access.

In 2026, an estimated 33.7 million people require humanitarian assistance. Millions have been displaced, while widespread food insecurity, malnutrition and disease threaten lives. Conflict has damaged essential infrastructure, limiting access to healthcare, education, clean water and sanitation.

Recent rainfall deficits have further worsened the already dire situation. Immediate life-saving assistance, including food, clean water, sanitation and healthcare, is urgently needed.
