**Description**

Afghanistan continues to face a severe humanitarian crisis in 2026, driven by poverty, food insecurity, climate-related disasters, economic instability, and restrictions on women and girls.

According to OCHA, approximately 21.9 million people require humanitarian assistance. Limited access to food, healthcare, clean water, and education further worsens living conditions. The return of Afghans from Pakistan and Iran has placed additional pressure on public services, while women, children, displaced people, and rural communities remain particularly vulnerable.

Insufficient international funding further limits humanitarian assistance. Addressing the crisis requires sustained international support, emergency aid, protection of women’s and girls’ rights, and long-term investment in essential services and livelihoods.

**Source**

OCHA, [Afghanistan: Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan 2026 (December 2025)](https://reliefweb.int/report/afghanistan/afghanistan-humanitarian-needs-and-response-plan-2026-december-2025-endarips)
