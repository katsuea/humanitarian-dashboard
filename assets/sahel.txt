**Description**

The Sahel is facing a deepening humanitarian crisis driven by armed conflict, mass displacement, climate shocks, food insecurity and economic hardship. 

Violence by armed groups is spreading across the region, disrupting livelihoods and access to essential services. Meanwhile, climate change is intensifying vulnerabilities through erratic rainfall, prolonged droughts and devastating floods, further undermining food security and community resilience.

More than 24 million people across the region need humanitarian assistance and protection. Growing displacement in placing increasing pressure on host communities, whose limited resources and access to basic serivces are already overstreched. 

As humanitarian need rise, deminishing international funding is forcing aid organisations to scale back to operations and prioritise the people in the most urgent need, leaving millions at risk of receiving insufficient assistance.
