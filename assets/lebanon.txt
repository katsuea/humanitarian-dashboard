**Description**

Lebanon faces a severe humanitarian crisis following renewed hostilities since 2 March 2026. Airstrikes, ground incursions and displacement orders have caused mass displacement, damaged infrastructure and disrupted essential services. Despite ceasefire announcements, hostilities continued, particularly in southern Lebanon, amid ongoing economic hardship and widespread poverty.

Humanitarian needs include emergency shelter, food, healthcare, safe water, sanitation and protection services. Damaged homes, disrupted livelihoods and limited access to essential services continue to hinder safe and sustainable returns, leaving displaced people and affected communities vulnerable.
