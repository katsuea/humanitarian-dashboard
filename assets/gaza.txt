**Description**

Gaza continues to face a severe humanitarian crisis. Since October 2023, tens of thousands of Palestinians, including more than 20,000 children, have been killed. Despite the October 2025 ceasefire, attacks have continued, with more than 1,400 Palestinians reportedly killed since its announcement.

Most of Gaza’s population has been displaced. Food shortages, destroyed homes and limited access to safe water and health care threaten lives, while restrictions on aid and funding shortages hinder relief efforts.

Sustained international support and safe humanitarian access are essential to deliver basic assistance to vulnerable families.
